About prices

Pokémon prices come from the Pokémon TCG API (TCGplayer market). Magic prices come from Scryfall (USD). Yu-Gi-Oh! prices come from YGOPRODeck (TCGPlayer). Anything else can be added with a manual price. Market figures are typically near-mint and can lag or be missing for some printings.
